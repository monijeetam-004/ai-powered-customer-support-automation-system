COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT - ABC Technologies

REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase if they are not satisfied with the service.
Annual subscription refunds are prorated based on unused months and require supervisor approval before processing.
Refunds for monthly subscriptions are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
No refunds are issued after 30 days except in cases of proven billing errors.

SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time from the account settings page.
Cancellation requests for annual plans must be reviewed by a supervisor to confirm eligibility for partial refunds.
Once cancelled, customer data is retained for 90 days before permanent deletion.

ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY
Account closure requests must be verified by a human supervisor to prevent accidental or fraudulent closures.
All outstanding invoices must be settled before an account can be closed.
Closed accounts can be reactivated within 90 days by contacting support.

COMPENSATION POLICY
Compensation requests (service credits, goodwill refunds) due to downtime or service issues must be approved by a supervisor.
Standard compensation is one month of service credit for outages exceeding 24 hours.

ESCALATION POLICY
Any request explicitly escalated to management must be reviewed by a human supervisor before a response is sent.
Escalations are typically resolved within 2 business days.

BILLING DISPUTES
Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the disputed charge.
Disputed charges are placed on hold pending investigation.
